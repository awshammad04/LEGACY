# Asset credits and usage

## Included material maps (CC0)

- **Aerial Sand** — Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_sand . Included: 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness.
- **Rock Boulder Dry** — Dimitrios Savva (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing) / Poly Haven. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry . Included: 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness.
- Asset licence: https://polyhaven.com/license — Poly Haven identifies its asset files as CC0. These are the downloaded asset maps, not website screenshots or preview renders. Attribution is retained for provenance.

The maps are Earth material scans used artistically for a fictional Mars landscape. They are not NASA terrain data, evidence of a real Martian location, or authentic historical equipment.

## Three.js (MIT)

Vendored Three.js r180 is included only to run this standalone kit and reference. Its complete licence is in vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt and reference-project/dist/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Retain the licence when redistributing the renderer. Use the larger project's existing engine dependency during integration rather than adding a second copy.

## Original graphics and adaptations

The fictional LGC–01 rover, atmosphere, terrain geometry, UI and adapters are supplied as editable project code. No NASA insignia or copyrighted Starfield assets/screenshots are used as production textures or models. The drivable rover is not Opportunity. Reference screenshots included in this kit were captured from the working LEGACY prototype.

No credential, hosting identity or runtime player save is needed to use these assets and modules.
